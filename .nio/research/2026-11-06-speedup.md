- 41 of 78 rounds found no new kept blocker or major. All of those are fix checks, which `nio jury changed` already limits to the lenses with findings. E-1's round 2 was a single juror that took 30 s.

## Answer: is the jury run too often?
**No, not by round count any more.**
- Rounds came down from about 3.9 to 2 per gate.
- The second round is already a cheap fix check that only re-runs lenses with findings, and it still catches unfixed findings (parallel-waves: 2 of 11 not fixed).
- Every lens finds real issues that no other lens raises.

The jury is slow and costly for three other reasons:
1. **Evidence re-runs** at the implementation gate: about 10–14 min per run, two runs per epic.
2. **Orchestrator overhead.** The orchestrator runs 15–41 turns per gate on 200–400k context, and those turns cost 2–10x the jurors.
3. **Re-emits** on format slips.

Outside the jury, the slow part is test runs inside workers, red and green.
